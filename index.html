Meet your captain

Captain John Callion

A proud 4th-generation Keys fishing family, with roots that run deep in the waters of Marathon. John was born and raised in the Keys and carries on a family tradition that began in the 1950s.

Together with Jack Callion, he has run Callion Fishing Charters since its founding in 1979. The family has won numerous tournaments for tarpon, bonefish, sailfish and shark, and has been featured on TV fishing shows alongside some of the biggest names in the sport. Book with a local, and fish the Keys the way the locals do.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book a trip?

Book online above with a deposit, or call or text Captain John at (305) 394-7991. You can also email Johncallionfishing@gmail.com.

How many people can come?

Up to 4 guests. The Special Flats trip is 2 anglers max.

When is tarpon season?

Afternoon tarpon trips run from March 20 and morning trips from April 1, both through June 30.

Do I need a fishing license?

No. Licenses for your whole party are covered, along with bait, rods and lures.

Can I keep my fish?

Yes. Captain John cleans and fillets your keeper fish when you're done.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. Trips are child friendly, and 95% of reviewers call the trip family friendly.

Where do we meet?

Callion Fishing Charters is at 11833 Overseas Highway in Marathon. Captain John confirms where to meet when you book.
